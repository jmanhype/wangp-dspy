Live measurements: wgp multishot concat trims ~2 frames per seam (470f out vs 474f expected, 3 shots). Frame readback tolerance now scales: 2 + MEASURED_SEAM_FRAMES*(n-1); future high-n mismatches are measurement requests, not bugs. Also: bare ssh channels can hang silently after remote completion — ServerAliveInterval=30/CountMax=10 drops dead channels in ~5min, plus exactly-one fetch retry, recovers the output without a manual scp.
