Live measurements: wgp multishot concat trims frames per seam SUPERLINEARLY, not linear-2 (WD-qn1a): n=3 diff 4 (~2.0/seam), n=4 diff 8 (~2.67/seam); the original linear-2 tolerance fired on a GOOD cycle-4 render. Frame readback tolerance now scales: 2 + MEASURED_SEAM_CEILING*3*(n-1)... see fix/seam-tolerance-recalibrate — 2 + 3*(n-1), recalibrate at an n>=6 data point. Future high-n mismatches are measurement requests, not bugs. Also: bare ssh channels can hang silently after remote completion — ServerAliveInterval=30/CountMax=10 drops dead channels in ~5min, plus exactly-one fetch retry, recovers the output without a manual scp.
